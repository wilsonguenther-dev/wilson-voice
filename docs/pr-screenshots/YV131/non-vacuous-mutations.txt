DISCLOSED GREEN — mutation 7, and what it actually tells us
=================================================================
Mutation: route the AS-norm trials through `enrollment_eer`, the COSINE-typed
entry point, whose `CosineSimilarity::new` clamps every input to [-1, 1].

Result: the suite stayed GREEN. The equal error rate is identical either way.

Why, and why the guard still ships:
  * Clamping is monotone non-decreasing, and on this arm the equal-error
    crossing lands INSIDE [-1, 1], where the ordering of scores survives it.
    The EER is a rank statistic, so it does not move.
  * That is a property of this cohort, this channel and this corpus — not a
    theorem. The crossing point is free to fall outside the surviving region
    for the next one, and then the clamp silently reports a wrong number in
    the one place this epic measures itself.
  * The inputs are emphatically not in range: 82.7% of the 2,178 committed
    scores lie outside [-1, 1], spanning -18.98 to +6.46. The clamp fuses
    them into ties at the boundary and discards the distribution.

So `the_measured_scores_do_not_fit_in_the_cosine_range` was added to assert the
part that is not luck: that the scores overflow the cosine range, and that
clamping visibly collapses the number of distinct values. That test IS red if
the normalized scores ever become cosine-shaped, which is the real regression
this guard is for.

Reported here rather than counted as a win, because a mutation that did not
fail is evidence about the test, and hiding it would make this file worthless.
