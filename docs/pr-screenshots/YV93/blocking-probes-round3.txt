PROBE 5 — the fixture proving itself (why probes 1 and 4 are not vacuous)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first cut of `StraddleDecoder` looked correct and was inert: window bounds
are read back off an `f32` ramp, so a window that really does stop at 90.000000 s
reconstructs as 90.000002 s, and against the 1e-6 tolerance it took the "I saw the
whole word" branch instead of emitting the truncated fragment. With no fragment
there is no truncated word at the seam, nothing to duplicate, and PROBE 1 passed
GREEN on a build carrying the bug.

`BOUNDARY_EPS = 1e-3` fixes it, and the test no longer takes the fixture on
trust: it asserts, before relying on any of it, that the outgoing copy ENDS at
the cut, that the incoming copy STARTS there, and that the two are DISJOINT —
disjointness being precisely what the intersection rule cannot see through and
the truncation rule can. It also asserts the ledger really carries a word ending
at the resume point.
