2. THE CLOCK RUNS FAST  (`elapsed.as_secs()` → `elapsed.as_millis() / 250`)
   — a counter dressed up as a clock. Caught by arithmetic, not by timing: the
   payload can never exceed the real time observed at the sink.

   thread '…' panicked at src-tauri/tests/meeting_manual_start_stop.rs:264:9:

   test result: FAILED. 0 passed; 1 failed
