meet Yappy — your dictation buddy

Yappy is a little chick who lives in your menu bar. It breathes, blinks, and hops on its own; when you dictate it perks up and its beak moves with your voice; while it thinks it gets impatient; when it's done it hops for joy and yaps a line based on how much you said. Hand-coded, no sprites — say hi below.
