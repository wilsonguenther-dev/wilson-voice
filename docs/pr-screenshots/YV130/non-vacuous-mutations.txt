── the zero-norm fix (added after the panel's BLOCKING finding) ──────────────
The finding was that `cosine_similarity` answers 0.0 for a zero-norm vector
meaning "no information", and `CosineDistance::from_similarity(0.0)` is 1.0
meaning "far" — so split read a degenerate embedding as the most distinctive
voice in the cluster. Both halves of the fix are broken separately below,
because they close different halves of the failure.
