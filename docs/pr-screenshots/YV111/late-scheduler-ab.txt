YV111 — the old shape vs the new one, against the SAME simulated starvation

The flake this item removes is a loaded CI runner failing to schedule the
controller's ticker thread often enough inside a 600 ms wall-clock window. A
12-core M-series laptop will not reproduce that by being loaded: the runs under
`load average 28` and `43` (ten-runs-under-load.txt) all passed with BOTH the old
and the new test, which is exactly why the flake only ever showed on macos-14 CI.

So the starvation was simulated instead — one temporary line in
`spawn_ticker`'s loop, `std::thread::sleep(tick * 2)`, i.e. "the scheduler ran
this thread three times later than it asked". Same patch, both tests, reverted
after.
