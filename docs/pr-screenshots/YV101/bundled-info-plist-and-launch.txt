YV101 — the Info.plist string and the untouched deployment floor,
read out of the BUNDLE, not out of the source file.

$ npx tauri build --bundles app
$ plutil -p Yap.app/Contents/Info.plist

  "CFBundleIdentifier" => "com.wilsonguenther.wilson-voice"
  "LSMinimumSystemVersion" => "12.0"
  "NSAppleEventsUsageDescription" => "Yap pastes transcribed text into the app you're typing in."
  "NSAudioCaptureUsageDescription" => "Yap records the audio from your meeting — the other people on the call as well as you — so it can write the notes. Recording stays on your Mac."
  "NSMicrophoneUsageDescription" => "Yap records your voice locally to transcribe dictation with Whisper. Audio never leaves your Mac."

LSMinimumSystemVersion is still 12.0 — this item is a runtime gate, not a
deployment-target bump. Raising the floor would 'fix' the launch failure by
dropping every macOS 12/13 user instead, which is what §2.1 refuses.

The weak-linked, ad-hoc-signed bundle binary LOADS and RUNS.
A dyld bind failure would abort before main; this reaches the
app's own headless CLI and reports the missing file.
==============================================================
