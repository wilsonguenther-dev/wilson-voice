question: at what span length does the extractor's answer stop being about the length
          and start being about the speaker?

--- method ---
For each of 18 utterances (6 say voices x 3 texts): embed the FULL utterance, then embed a
CENTRED window of T seconds of the same rendering and score the window against the full-length
vector. Centred, not a prefix, so leading silence is not what is being measured. The bar is the
SAME roster's global impostor mean (0.5789): below it, a window resembles its own full-length
vector less than an average stranger does.
