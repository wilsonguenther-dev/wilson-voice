This is the weekly planning note for the garden club. We met on Saturday morning at the community hall and talked through the spring schedule, the budget, and the volunteer rota. Everyone agreed that the season went well last year and that we should keep most of the same plans. The main change is that we want to start the seed swap two weeks earlier so that people have more time to plant. We also want to move the plant sale to the car park because the hall gets crowded by eleven. The weather last spring was kind to us, although the late frost in April cost us most of the early beans. This year we will cover the beds with fleece at night until the middle of May. Before the first workday there are a few jobs to finish. Make sure the tool shed is unlocked. Make sure the compost bins are emptied. Make sure the hoses are connected to the tap by the gate. The treasurer gave a short report on the money side of things, and the numbers look healthy for the first time in a while. We still need to buy the new labels, the seed trays, the twine, the gloves, and a second wheelbarrow, and we will look for a good price on all of them before the next meeting. For the volunteer rota we need four groups, the watering group, the weeding group, the planting group, the harvest group. People can sign up for more than one group if they like, and nobody has to commit to every week. The watering group will need the most people in July and August when the beds dry out quickly. We talked about a small rain tank behind the shed, and the treasurer thinks we can afford one if the plant sale goes well. The school has asked whether the children can visit in June to see the vegetable beds and plant a row of sunflowers. We said yes, and we will ask two volunteers to show them around and answer questions. The newsletter will go out at the end of the month with the full schedule and a sign up form. If anyone has photos from last year, please send them to the secretary so we can use them on the notice board. The next meeting is on the second Saturday of next month at ten in the morning, and there will be tea and cake as usual. Thank you to everyone who came along and helped to plan the year.
